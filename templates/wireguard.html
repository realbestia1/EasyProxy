            WireGuard profile
        

        
        

            
            
            
        

        

            Needs [Interface] with PrivateKey and Address plus a [Peer] with
            PublicKey and Endpoint. IPv6 addresses and wg-quick-only directives (Table, PostUp...) are stripped before the tunnel starts.
            Missing DNS, MTU and PersistentKeepalive are filled with 1.1.1.1,
            1420 and 25; your own values are kept.
        

    


    

        

            
            Use it in EasyProxy
        

        
Paste this URL into Global Proxies, a Transport Route or an extractor proxy (remote DNS through the tunnel):

        socks5h://127.0.0.1:1082
        

            Cloudflare WARP already listens on 127.0.0.1:1080, so this tunnel uses its own port.
            Change it above if you need something else. TCP only: the SOCKS5 endpoint cannot carry UDP.
